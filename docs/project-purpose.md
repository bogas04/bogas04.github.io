# Project purpose

`bogas04.fyi` is the author's personal place on the web. It exists to express a
personal digital identity on the author's own domain rather than depend on a
corporate platform.

The site can include personal and professional writing, image galleries,
portfolio material such as work experience, education, talks, and projects, and
new facets as the author decides they belong here.

Do not narrow the site into a conventional portfolio or blog. New features are
welcome when they let the author own more of their expression or data, but they
need an explicit product and visual decision from the author.
